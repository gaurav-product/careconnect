# Product overview

## The one line

> **CareConnect is the care record that stays with the patient, not the agency.**

## What it is

A web app that turns the first 30 days after a hospital discharge into a record a family
can read. The family builds a care plan from their discharge summary. The attendant they
already employ records each shift on their own phone, in Hindi and English, in under five
minutes. Anything outside the plan raises a deterministic alert the family must close with
a note. When the attendant is replaced — the normal case in this workforce — a handover
pack carries the plan, the week and every decision already taken to whoever comes next.

**CareConnect supplies no caregivers.** That is a strategy, not a gap.

## Who it is for

**The adult child living in another city** who is arranging and paying for home care after
a parent's discharge. Not the elder (not a user), not the agency (structurally conflicted),
not the hospital (a different sale entirely).

## The problem

> A family can hire a home attendant within a day, but has no way to check that the day's
> care actually happened, no early warning when it did not, and nothing that survives the
> attendant being replaced.

## The hypothesis being tested

> Can a simple shared care record improve visibility and continuity for families managing
> post-discharge home care?

**Not** being tested: clinical outcomes, diagnosis, treatment efficacy, marketplace demand,
or anything requiring statistical significance.

## The three roles

| Role | What they do | What they need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Family** | Creates the case and the plan, invites the attendant, reads the record, closes alerts | Visibility, continuity, and to know what was *not* done |
| **Attendant** | Records each shift — the primary data-entry user | Speed, their own language, and a legitimate way to say "could not" |
| **Patient** | The subject of the record | Not an application user. See the consent limitation below |

## What it deliberately is not

Not a diagnosis tool. Not medical advice. Not an emergency service. Not a caregiver
marketplace. Not a surveillance product — no GPS, no geofencing, no camera, no microphone,
no facial recognition, no health score, no AI prediction.

**Documentation is not verification.** CareConnect records what an identified person entered,
at a time, under their name, with corrections kept. It cannot prove a tablet was swallowed
or a walk happened, and it never claims to.
