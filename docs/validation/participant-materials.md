# Participant materials

Everything a participant sees or is told during the pilot. **Nothing here has been used
with a real person.**

Two principles run through all of it: say what the product does *not* do as clearly as what
it does, and never ask a participant for information the research question does not need.

---

## 1 · Attendant one-pager

Printed on one side of A4, handed over at setup. Hindi first, because the reader is
Hindi-first; English below each line for a shared phone and a supervisor's glance.

> ### CareConnect — आपके लिए / for you
>
> **यह क्या है? · What is this?**
> परिवार ने एक ऐप बनाया है जहाँ आप हर ड्यूटी में यह लिखते हैं कि आपने क्या किया।
> *The family has set up an app where you record what you did on each shift.*
>
> **मुझे क्या करना है? · What do I do?**
> 1. ड्यूटी शुरू करें दबाएँ। *Tap Start my shift.*
> 2. दवा दी → **दे दी** दबाएँ। नहीं दी → **नहीं दी** दबाएँ और एक लाइन में कारण लिखें।
>    *Medicine given → tap Given. Not given → tap Not given and write one line saying why.*
> 3. काम हो गया → **हो गया**। नहीं हो पाया → **नहीं हुआ** और कारण।
>    *Task done → Done. Couldn't → Could not, and the reason.*
> 4. कुछ गड़बड़ लगे → **समस्या बताएं**। *Something wrong → Report a problem.*
> 5. जाते समय → **ड्यूटी खत्म** और अगले व्यक्ति के लिए एक नोट।
>    *Leaving → End shift, and a note for the next person.*
>
> **"नहीं हुआ" लिखना गलत नहीं है। · "Could not" is not a wrong answer.**
> अगर मरीज़ ने मना किया, दवा खत्म थी, या समय नहीं मिला — वही लिखें। यही सबसे काम की
> जानकारी है।
> *If the patient refused, the strip was finished, or there was no time — write that.
> It is the most useful thing you can record.*
>
> **परिवार क्या देख सकता है? · What can the family see?**
> सिर्फ़ वही जो आप लिखते हैं, समय, और आपका नाम। **लोकेशन नहीं, कैमरा नहीं, माइक नहीं।**
> *Only what you record, the time, and your name. No location, no camera, no microphone.*
>
> **यह डॉक्टर नहीं है। · This is not a doctor.**
> आपात स्थिति में पहले एम्बुलेंस और परिवार को कॉल करें — ऐप में मत लिखें।
> *In an emergency call an ambulance and the family first — do not stop to type.*
>
> **अगर सेव न हो · If it does not save**
> लाल पट्टी दिखेगी। नेटवर्क आने पर वही बटन दोबारा दबाएँ।
> *A red banner appears. Tap the same button again when the signal returns.*
>
> कुछ समझ न आए तो पूछें: **[researcher name, phone]**
> *Ask if anything is unclear — and if it is confusing, that is our mistake, not yours.*

## 2 · Family one-pager

> ### CareConnect — what you're trying out
>
> **What it is.** A shared record of what happened at home each day, kept by the attendant
> you already employ. You see it wherever you are.
>
> **What it is not.**
> - It is **not** a monitoring service. Nobody is watching your parent.
> - It **cannot confirm** that a tablet was swallowed or a walk happened. It records what
>   the attendant entered, at a time, under their name. That is documentation, not proof.
> - It is **not an emergency service**, and alerts are **not delivered to your phone in this
>   trial** — you see them when you open the app. Keep every arrangement you already have
>   for emergencies exactly as it is.
> - It gives **no medical advice**. Decisions stay with your doctor.
>
> **What we'd like you to do.** Use it, or don't. Carry on exactly as you would have.
> We are trying to find out whether it earns its place, and the honest answer might be no.
>
> **What we'll ask you.** Fifteen minutes on day 3, half an hour on day 14.
>
> **Your data.** Only the people you invite to this care plan can see it. We look at
> anonymised usage counts — never your parent's notes or readings. You can ask us to delete
> everything at any point, including after the trial, and we will.

## 3 · Consent scripts

Spoken, not signed. A form in this setting reads as officialdom and changes what people
tell you. Record in the log that the script was read and that the person agreed.

**For the family**

> This is research, not a service. We're trying to find out whether this is useful, and it
> might not be — that result is just as valuable to us. Nothing you say goes to your
> attendant or their agency. I'll write about what I learn without your name or your
> parent's name. You can stop at any point, skip any question, or ask me to delete
> everything, including after we finish. Can I record our conversations, just for my notes?

**For the attendant**

> I don't work for your agency and I'm not reporting to them. Nothing you say to me reaches
> your employer or the family. You're being paid for this time whether or not you use the
> app, and whether or not you like it — if you tell me it's useless, you still get paid and
> nothing happens to your job. You can stop at any point. Is that clear? Do you want to ask
> me anything before we start?

That last paragraph is the one to slow down on. An attendant who believes their job depends
on cooperating will cooperate, and the pilot will learn nothing.

**For the patient** — spoken by the family, in the patient's own language, with the
researcher present if the family wants:

> The person helping you is going to write down what they do each day, on their phone, so
> [name] can see it from [city]. It's about their work, not about watching you. Is that all
> right with you?

If the patient says no, that household does not enter the pilot. No persuasion.

### Observation session protocol

Two sessions per participant, 30 minutes, in their own home or workplace, on their own
phone.

- Open with: *"I'm testing the product, not you. If something is confusing that's my
  mistake, and finding it is the whole point."*
- **Never coach.** No pointing, no "try the green one". If asked what to do, reply *"what
  would you do if I weren't here?"* and wait.
- Ask them to think aloud, then be quiet. Count silently to ten before saying anything.
- Intervene only after 60 seconds of being genuinely stuck — and record it as a **task
  failure**, not as a hint given.
- Record per task: completed unaided (yes/no) · time · errors · times they asked for help ·
  where their eyes went first · whether they read the Hindi or the English label.
